This Privacy Policy applies to PixelRevive – AI Photo Enhancer ("PixelRevive", "the app", "we", "us", or "our"). PixelRevive provides AI-powered photo enhancement features such as improving image clarity, sharpness, and quality.

By using PixelRevive, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.
