Wallpapers and Content Delivery

Hosting & Content Delivery: The wallpaper library available in ScreenMe AI comprises curated collections and AI-assisted artworks hosted on secure cloud storage and distributed via a Content Delivery Network (CDN) to ensure fast loading times globally.

Downloads & Device Storage: When you choose to download or set a wallpaper:

  • The full-resolution image file is downloaded directly to your device.
  • On Android 10 (API level 29) and higher, ScreenMe AI utilizes Android’s standard scoped storage APIs (MediaStore) to place the image into your device’s public Pictures or Wallpaper directory without requiring broad device storage permissions.
  • On legacy Android versions, the Application may request the standard WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE permission strictly to save requested wallpaper images.
  • Downloaded files remain on your device even after you close or uninstall the Application until you manually delete them from your gallery.

Intellectual Property & Content Verification: We strive to ensure wallpaper content respects intellectual property. If you believe any wallpaper accessible within the Application infringes your intellectual property, please consult Section 8 of our Terms and Conditions for our DMCA / Copyright complaint procedure.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

ScreenMe AI is a native mobile application built for Android. The native application itself does not use traditional web browser cookies (such as HTTP session cookies) for core navigation or wallpaper rendering.

Instead, the Application relies on native mobile application storage mechanisms:

  • Android SharedPreferences / Local SQLite: Used to save UI settings, dark theme toggles, and user favorites locally.
  • Device Cache Directory: Used temporarily by image loading libraries to cache downloaded wallpaper thumbnails and prevent excessive network bandwidth consumption.
  • Mobile SDK Identifiers: Used by advertising or analytics SDKs (if implemented) as detailed in Section 6 and Section 7.

If the Application opens an embedded browser view (Custom Tabs or WebView) to display external links, third-party cookie policies of the visited website will apply.
