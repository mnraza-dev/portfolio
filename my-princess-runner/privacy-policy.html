Introduction

Welcome to My Princess Runner ("My Princess Runner", "the Application", "we", "us", or "our"). My Princess Runner is a casual mobile game application designed for Android devices.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you download or use My Princess Runner, why we collect it, and how we handle it. We are committed to protecting your privacy and your personal data.

This policy applies solely to the My Princess Runner mobile application and does not extend to third-party websites, applications, or services that may be linked within the game.

2

Information We Collect

We collect information to provide, improve, and maintain our game. We only collect information that is necessary for the game to function properly.

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Game Progress and Save Data: We store your game progress, high scores, achievements, and preferences locally on your device to save your progress.
  • User Profile Information (Optional): If you choose to create a profile, we may collect your username or nickname.
  • Support Communications: If you contact our support, we receive your email address and the contents of your message.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, app version, and screen specifications. This helps us optimize the game for different devices.
  • Gameplay Analytics: Aggregated data about how you play the game (levels completed, features used). This helps us improve the game experience.
  • Crash Data: Information about crashes or errors to help us fix bugs and improve stability.

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Provide the Game: To deliver and let you enjoy My Princess Runner, including storing your game progress and save data.
  • Improve the Game: To analyze gameplay patterns and fix bugs to enhance your experience.
  • Communicate: To respond to your questions, feedback, or support requests.
  • Product Updates: To notify you about new versions or important updates to the game.

Data Storage and Security

We take the security of your information seriously. We employ commercially reasonable measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.
